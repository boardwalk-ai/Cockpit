# Panel 10 — Research & Explanations (ON-P10, full specification)

Authoritative spec. `MEMORY.md` holds the condensed status. Panel 10 handles
**evidence gathering + source comparison**; Panel 9 handles faster AI
explanations and commands. Course materials are searched **first**, then trusted
external sources, always preserving evidence, provenance, and student approval.

## 1. Main goals
Research a selected term/question/claim/diagram/note · course materials first ·
expand externally when needed · know which source supports each finding · compare
explanations · detect disagreement · open the exact slide/page/timestamp · insert
approved explanations · save research without inserting · preserve for Review +
Study Studio · keep taking notes while research runs.

## 2. Panel 9 vs Panel 10
Panel 9: fast explanations/transformations, existing context, short refs, routes
research onward, live-optimized. Panel 10: multi-source evidence, course+external
search, detailed source cards, executes the workflow, can run long/background.
"Explain ATP synthase" → P9; "Research why oxygen is required and show reliable
sources" → P10.

## 3. Entry points
Research tab · Research on a selected block / handwriting / transcript segment ·
Research This Claim on a slide · Research Further from Panel 9 · a Smart
Organization evidence warning · direct query. The originating context is attached
on open.

## 4. Panel structure
Research question · context chips · scope selector · search progress · synthesized
explanation · evidence/source cards · conflicts/limitations · insert/save controls
· follow-up field.

## 5. Research context
Selected block(s) · handwriting · formula · diagram · transcript segment/range ·
slide · PDF excerpt · board photo · current topic · whole session. Chips stay
visible: `Researching with: Student Question · Transcript · 19:02–19:08 · Week 4
Slides · Slide 7 · Biology 101`. Removable/addable before searching.

## 6. Scope selector
- **Course Sources** (default) — current slides, PDFs/handouts, transcript, typed
  notes, handwriting recognition, related course files, authorized Deck materials.
- **Academic & Official Sources** — primary/official orgs, academic publications,
  textbooks, university resources, recognized educational refs.
- **Broader Web** — general web when other scopes are insufficient.
External research requires a visible scope selection or escalation.

## 7. Course-material-first workflow
Interpret question → search selected material → search session → search related
course/Deck material → evaluate whether evidence answers it → **ask before
expanding externally** when avoidable → search trusted external → synthesize with
source links → **wait for approval** before inserting.

## 8. Research depth
Quick Explanation (few highly relevant sources, fast, live-safe) · Standard
Research (multiple course+external, supported explanation) · Deep Research (broad
set, finds disagreements/limitations, async, explicit activation only — not for
every definition).

## 9–11. Query + result + example
Result shows: Direct answer · Key findings · Course context (professor/slide/
handout) · External support · Source agreement · Limitations. Example explanation:
"Oxygen is required indirectly because it acts as the final electron acceptor in
the electron transport chain. Without oxygen, electrons cannot continue moving,
the proton gradient collapses, and NADH cannot be efficiently converted back to
NAD⁺. Glycolysis can continue temporarily through fermentation, but aerobic ATP
production cannot." Each major claim connects to supporting sources.

## 12. Evidence cards
Each card: source title · source type · author/publisher · publication date ·
relevant page/slide/timestamp · short excerpt · AI explanation · quality indicator
· Open Source · Save Source · Use in Notes. **The original excerpt stays visually
separate from the AI summary.**

## 13. Source types
Course Slide · Lecture Transcript · Course Handout · Textbook · Academic Paper ·
Official Source · University Source · Educational Website · General Web Source ·
Student Note (context only, not independent external evidence).

## 14. Quality hierarchy
Course materials → professor's lecture → primary/official → academic papers →
textbooks → university resources → reputable educational sites → general web →
user-generated. Quality is contextual (a course slide may be the most relevant for
what will be examined).

## 15. Traceability
Course sources resolve precisely (`Transcript · 19:08`, `Week 4 Slides · Slide 7`,
`Handout · Page 3`, `Magic Pencil · Page 3`, `Typewriter Block · ETC Section`).
External keep title, author, publisher, URL, publication + accessed date, page/
section, excerpt.

## 16. External-source behavior
Label "External Source"; never appear as professor / course materials / student
notes / raw transcript / original slides. Student can restrict to course-only,
external-only, or both.

## 17. Claim-level grounding
Don't attach one source list to a whole long answer. Each key finding shows source
markers: "Oxygen acts as the final electron acceptor. Sources: Transcript 19:08 ·
Slide 7 · OpenStax Biology". Selecting a marker opens its evidence card.

## 18. Source conflicts
Show a visible conflict card ("Your note: '4 ATP net.' Lecture: '2 ATP net.' Slide
7: '2 ATP net.'"). Actions: Open Student Note · Play Transcript · Open Slide ·
Research Further · Correct Note · Keep Note. Explain disagreements, don't silently
pick.

## 19. Course vs external nuance
Distinguish what the course teaches vs what broader research says vs what to
preserve for class vs extra nuance. "For this course: use the professor's
definition. Additional context: external sources describe a broader mechanism."

## 20. Low-evidence result
Say "The attached course material does not provide enough information to answer
this confidently." Actions: Search External Sources · Ask a Narrower Question · Add
More Material · Keep as Unresolved Question. Never manufacture a confident answer.

## 21. Insertion controls
Never auto-insert. Add as Explanation · Insert Below · Add Source Card · Link to
Current Block · Save to Research · Send to Typewriter · Attach to Magic Pencil ·
Add to Study Studio Selection · Dismiss. Avoid Replace Selection (research is
supporting material, not a rewrite tool).

## 22. Inserted research blocks
Contain: explanation · Research label · source markers · related student block ·
transcript timestamp · slide ref · creation time · research-request ID. "Research
Explanation · Linked to: Student Question · 19:02 · Sources: Transcript 19:08 ·
Slide 7 · OpenStax Biology".

## 23. Saved research
Save without inserting; later reopen/insert/compare/add to Panel 13/use in Study
Studio/link to a block/dismiss/archive. History grouped by question or source
context, not a chat.

## 24. Follow-ups
"Explain more simply." · "Use only course sources." · "Find a real-world example."
· "Show an academic source." · "Why do these sources disagree?" · "Compare aerobic
and anaerobic." · "Find what's most likely on the exam." Inherit the source set
unless changed.

## 25. Live-session behavior
Closed unless opened · results run in background · completed = subtle badge ·
never move the cursor · external pages don't replace the workspace · long requests
cancellable · typing/recording continue.

## 26. AI explanation vs evidence
Distinguish source excerpt (what the source says) vs AI explanation (system
interpretation) vs student-approved note. Never visually identical.

## 27. Panel connections
P3 hosts the tab; P5 supplies blocks + receives explanations; P6 supplies
handwriting/formulas/diagrams; P7 supplies timestamped evidence; P8 supplies
slides/PDFs; P9 routes deeper research; P11 uses approved research for Smart Notes;
P13 reviews retained research; P15 can include research in Study Studio; The Deck
supplies authorized materials + persistent source links.

## 28. Responsive
Desktop/web: Research tab, expands wider for comparison, notes stay visible, cards
open in a secondary preview. Tablet: side drawer/sheet, stacked cards, ink visible
behind. Mobile: full-screen sheet, direct answer first, cards expand individually,
touch-safe inserts.

## 29. Required interface states
Empty · context attached · course search running · external requested/running ·
Quick Explanation · Deep Research running · results ready · saved research ·
selected evidence · source conflict · sources agree · low evidence · no results ·
external unavailable · paywalled · source removed · permission denied · offline ·
queued · cancelled · error · rate limit · read-only.

## 30. Offline
Cached course materials searchable; saved research viewable; external unavailable;
queue an external request (query + context preserved); results return as
uninserted suggestions after reconnect; nothing auto-inserts.

## 31. Privacy
Authorized content only · show what context is sent externally · exclude private
notes from external queries unless necessary · minimum required info in external
queries · saved research inherits permissions · sharing notes doesn't share
restricted source files · analytics exclude raw note/transcript content.

## 32. Data structure
- **ResearchRequest** — Request ID · Session ID · query · context refs · scope ·
  depth · status · created/completion time.
- **ResearchSource** — Source ID · type · title · author · publisher · URL/internal
  ref · publication + accessed date · page/slide/section/timestamp · excerpt ·
  quality metadata · permission state.
- **ResearchFinding** — Finding ID · request ID · claim · explanation · supporting/
  contradicting source IDs · confidence · limitation · approval state.
- **ResearchInsertion** — destination block · insertion type · approved finding
  IDs · source refs · student action · revisions.

## 33. Architecture
Separate: context resolver · course-source index · Deck retrieval · external
connector · source extraction · source ranking · evidence synthesis · conflict
detection · provenance storage · research history · approved-insertion service.
Reuse existing search/extraction where appropriate; store results as OctoNotes
research objects linked to exact note/slide/ink/transcript refs.

## 34. Performance
Course results first · external async · first useful result doesn't wait for all
sources · progressive card loading · never block typing/handwriting/recording ·
cache duplicate searches · indexed text for large files · closing the tab keeps
the request.

## 35. Priority
- **P0** — direct query · context chips · course + external search · source cards ·
  supported explanation · claim-level markers · Insert Below · Add as Explanation ·
  Save Research · approval requirement · history · offline/error states.
- **P1** — Deep Research · quality scoring · conflict detection · course-vs-external
  comparison · Deck-wide search · academic/official filters · paywall handling ·
  Study Studio selection · source versioning · cross-note reuse.

## 36. Acceptance criteria
Course before external · student sees the researched context · every important
claim links to evidence · excerpts and AI explanations stay distinct · external
clearly labeled · conflicts visible · insufficient evidence → uncertainty not
invention · nothing inserts without approval · inserted research retains sources ·
runs without disrupting the lecture · offline failure doesn't affect notes · saved
findings remain for Panel 13 review.
